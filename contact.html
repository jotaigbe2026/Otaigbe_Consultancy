Discuss Your Challenge

Tell us what is happening, what it is costing and what decision is waiting on it. You will get a direct answer on whether independent technical review is warranted — including when it is not.

What happens next

  • You hear back within one business day. From the principal, not from a scheduler.
  • A short conversation, around twenty minutes. Enough to establish what the problem is and whether it needs an outside view.
  • A written scope before any work starts. What would be investigated, how, and what it would take.
  • An honest answer if the answer is no. Some problems belong with your own team or your resin supplier’s technical service group, and you will be told so.
